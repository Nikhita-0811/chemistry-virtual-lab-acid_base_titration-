# 🧪 Virtual Chemistry Laboratory – Acid-Base Titration

An interactive, web-based Virtual Chemistry Laboratory designed to help students understand and perform the **Acid-Base Titration experiment** through a realistic digital laboratory environment.

The application simulates the determination of the unknown concentration of Hydrochloric Acid (HCl) using a standard Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) solution. It provides students with a hands-on virtual learning experience without the need for physical laboratory equipment.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To provide an engaging, interactive and student-friendly virtual laboratory where learners can perform acid-base titration, understand neutralisation reactions, observe indicator colour changes, record experimental readings and calculate the concentration of an unknown acid.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Virtual Laboratory Environment:** Simulates a chemistry laboratory with essential apparatus such as burette, pipette, conical flask, beaker and reagent bottles.
- **Interactive Experiment:** Enables students to perform titration through virtual laboratory operations.
- **Real-Time Colour Changes:** Demonstrates the colour transition of phenolphthalein indicator during titration.
- **Burette Reading Simulation:** Allows students to observe and record initial and final burette readings.
- **pH Monitoring:** Displays changes in pH during the neutralisation process.
- **Observation Table:** Provides a structured space to record experimental readings and calculate the volume of titrant used.
- **Automatic Calculations:** Helps determine the unknown concentration using the titration formula.
- **Chemical Equation:** Demonstrates the neutralisation reaction between HCl and NaOH.
- **Learning Resources:** Includes experimental aim, theory, apparatus, procedure and precautions.
- **Assessment Module:** Provides interactive quizzes, viva-voce questions and knowledge checks.
- **Student-Friendly Interface:** Designed to make chemistry learning engaging and accessible.

## 🔬 Experiment Details

**Experiment:** Determination of the Concentration of Hydrochloric Acid using Standard Sodium Hydroxide Solution.

**Aim:** To determine the molarity of an unknown Hydrochloric Acid solution by titrating it against 0.1 M Sodium Hydroxide solution.

### Chemicals Required
- Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)
- Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) – 0.1 M
- Phenolphthalein indicator
- Distilled water

### Chemical Reaction

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

### Principle

The experiment is based on the neutralisation reaction between a strong acid and a strong base. Phenolphthalein acts as an indicator and changes from colourless to pale pink at the endpoint.

### Calculation Formula

**M₁V₁ = M₂V₂**

Where:

- M₁ = Molarity of HCl
- V₁ = Volume of HCl
- M₂ = Molarity of NaOH
- V₂ = Volume of NaOH consumed

## 🚀 Learning Outcomes

After completing the virtual experiment, students will be able to:

1. Identify and understand the functions of different titration apparatus.
2. Explain the principle of acid-base neutralisation.
3. Understand the role of indicators in titration.
4. Identify the endpoint of a titration.
5. Record and interpret burette readings.
6. Perform molarity calculations.
7. Develop scientific observation and analytical skills.
